# Shared Refund Credits

> Share a refund on a household bill fairly, without doing the maths yourselves

Added in version 3.5.0.

When a supplier refunds or credits a shared bill (an energy rebate, a water overpayment), the money belongs to both of you, but it usually lands with one person. A refund credit records who holds that money. The app then uses it on your household bills and works out what each of you owes.

## 1. Mark your household bills

Go to **Settings → Categories** and set each category to **Household bill** or **Not a bill**.

- Only **50/50** expenses in a household-bill category can use refund credit. Personal expenses and expenses one person owes in full never do.
- Nothing is marked until you choose. Until at least one category is a household bill, the Settlement page shows a reminder.
- A category can't be switched back to *Not a bill* while unsettled bills in it are using credit.

## 2. Record a refund

On the **Settlement** page, find **Shared refund credits** and choose **Record refund**. Enter:

| Field                         | What it means                                                                        |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Amount                        | The full refund, in £                                                                |
| Refund received on            | The date the money arrived. Only bills on or after this date can use it              |
| Who received it               | Who holds the money                                                                  |
| What it was for               | For example "Octopus Energy refund"                                                  |
| Use it on bills already added | On by default. Puts the credit towards household bills already added from that date, in months not yet settled, oldest bill first |

Either of you can record, change or remove a credit.

## 3. How credit is used

- **Automatically on new bills.** When a household bill is added, either on its own or from a recurring bill (generated by hand or by the daily run), open credit is used on it straight away.
- **Oldest credit first.** If there are several credits, the one received earliest is used first. A bill only uses credit received on or before its date.
- **Half each.** Using £X of credit on a bill means the holder owes the other person half of £X. That amount is added to the month's settlement, worked out in whole pence. Once a credit is fully used, the halves always add up exactly, even for an odd number of pence.
- **New bills only.** Editing a bill never adds more credit to it. If an edit lowers the amount, changes the split, moves it to a non-bill category or dates it before the refund, the app gives back the credit that no longer fits.

## 4. Managing a credit

Each credit on the Settlement page shows its status (*In use*, *Not in use* or *Used up*), how much is used and left, and the other person's half of what is left. Its menu offers:

- **Use on open bills.** Spread what is left over open household bills, oldest first.
- **Stop using on new bills / Use again on new bills.** Pause the credit. Bills it already covers keep their credit. Starting it again affects future bills (and *Use on open bills*) only.
- **Cash out what is left.** Settle the rest in money instead. The holder pays the other person their half as part of the month you choose, which must not be settled yet.
- **Edit** or **Remove.** Remove is not available once the credit has been used in a settled month.

**History** lists every bill and cash-out the credit was used on. In unsettled months you can **Release** (stop using it on that bill) or **Undo** a cash-out.

### On a bill

On the Dashboard, a bill covered by credit shows a **Credit £x** badge, and **View** lists the credits it used. Its actions include:

- **Use refund credit.** Add open credit to this bill, oldest first.
- **Stop using refund credit on this bill.** Give the credit back. The automatic process will not put it back on this bill; use **Use refund credit** if you change your mind.

## 5. What you'll see in settlements

- **Amounts paid stay as the full bills.** Credit never changes what was spent. The month's settlement card shows a separate **Refund credit** section explaining each adjustment.
- **Unused credit is separate.** Under **Unsettled months**, *Unused refund credit* shows who holds what is left and how much of it is the other person's. It is **not** part of the amount owed until it is used or cashed out.
- **Settled months are protected.** After a month is settled, its bills and the credit used in it can't be changed, released or removed. The app tells you which month to unsettle first.

## 6. Activity

The **Feed** has a **Refunds** filter showing when credit was recorded, used (by you or automatically), stopped, closed, reopened or cashed out.

## Example

Jamie receives a £100.01 energy refund on 20 October and records it, held by Jamie.

1. The November electricity bill (£96.37, paid by Antonio) uses £96.37 of it. Jamie owes Antonio an extra £48.18 that month.
2. A water bill uses the remaining £3.64. Jamie owes an extra £1.82. Altogether Jamie passes on £50.00 and keeps £50.01; an odd penny can't be split.
3. The credit now shows **Used up**. Later bills don't use it.
